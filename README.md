Kitchen Rush — a fast cooking game: orders come in on the board, you grab ingredients from the counter and serve dishes at the window before the shift ends. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best cooking game” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Play

- **Move the chef** — ← → or A D, or click where he should walk
- **Grab an ingredient** — SPACE near its bin, keys 1–6, or click the bin
- **Serve a dish** — SPACE at the serving window when the tray holds exactly what an order asks for

Serve 8 dishes before the 2-minute shift ends to win. A wrong dish costs a life — three wrong dishes end the shift. Every served dish adds 5 seconds to the clock, and serving dishes in a row builds a streak bonus.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
